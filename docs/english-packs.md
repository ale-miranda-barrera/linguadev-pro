**Gramática (bloques):**

- **Present simple vs. present continuous** — Present simple = rutinas/hechos. Present continuous = acción en este momento o temporal.
  - _ej.:_ I work from home on Fridays.
  - _ej.:_ I'm working on a new project this week.
  - Práctica:
    - Complete: She ___ (study) English every day. → **studies**
    - Complete: Right now I ___ (read) a book. → **am reading**
- **Past simple (regular vs. irregular)** — Regulares: +ed. Irregulares: forma propia (go → went, have → had, take → took).
  - _ej.:_ I worked late yesterday.
  - _ej.:_ She went to the store.
  - Práctica:
    - Complete: They ___ (go) to Rome last summer. → **went**
    - Complete: We ___ (watch) a movie last night. → **watched**
